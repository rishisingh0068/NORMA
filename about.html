Our organization

Introducing NORMA.

NORMA is a brand of NORMA DND PRODUCTS PRIVATE LIMITED, a business focused on healthcare, compression and medical-support products. The portfolio brings together garments, soft orthotics and patient-care products for a variety of support requirements.

A key area of the company's work is custom-made compression: garments designed from individual measurements, with attention to the area that needs coverage and the way the wearer moves. Alongside these measured products, the catalogue includes support belts, braces, foot-care products and equipment for patient-care environments.

What we do

Product support, from selection to enquiry.

Compression garments

The range includes graduated compression stockings, arm sleeves, gloves and garments for different areas of the body. Post-liposuction garments and abdominal binders offer additional design options. Individual measurements help determine the fit and coverage of custom-made products.

Explore compression products

Orthotic & foot care

Orthotic products include lumbo-sacral belts, Taylor's braces, knee supports and sternal-support options. Silicone insoles and Feather diabetic socks extend the range into foot care, with different profiles, materials and construction features to discuss with the treating healthcare professional.

Explore orthotic support

Patient-care products

The patient-care portfolio includes pneumatic ripple beds, PSP gel positioners and the Veinoplus calf-muscle stimulation device. These products have different configurations and intended uses; enquiries can cover the required setting, model, dimensions and current availability.

Our business focus

Connecting product information with practical needs.

Our website helps healthcare professionals, individual customers and business partners understand the NORMA range and start a focused enquiry. Clear product categories make it easier to compare design options and identify the information needed before an order is discussed.

For individual customers

Explore the available ranges and ask about sizes, measurements, wearing instructions and product care. Your treating healthcare professional can guide the selection of the appropriate support product.

For healthcare professionals

Review garment designs and support configurations, then contact the team with the relevant measurements or product requirements. Discuss fitting questions and the options listed in the catalogue.

For business partners

Contact the company about product information, availability and business requirements. Include the product category, location and scope of your enquiry so that the team has the context to respond.

Custom-made support

From individual measurements to a considered fit.

Made-to-measure garments begin with an understanding of the required design and coverage. NORMA's compression range uses elasticated cotton with L.B. Stretch, a fabric construction designed to accommodate movement in multiple directions.

1. Discuss the requirement

Identify the relevant product and coverage with the treating professional. Share the selected design and any fitting questions with the company.

2. Confirm measurements

Ask for the measurement instructions for the chosen garment. Accurate measurements provide the basis for the digitally designed, custom-made fit.

3. Review fitting and care

Confirm the supplied wearing and washing instructions. If fit or measurements change, contact the team to discuss the appropriate next steps.
