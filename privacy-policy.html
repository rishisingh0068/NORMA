Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NORMA DND PRODUCTS PRIVATE LIMITED ("NORMA", "we", "us" or "our") may collect, use and safeguard information when you visit this website or contact us through the website.

1. Information We Collect

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, organization name, enquiry subject and message content.

2. Information Submitted Through Forms

The form displays an enquiry preview in your browser. It does not send your message or store it on a website server.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • To respond to product, business or general enquiries.
  • To provide requested information or customer assistance.

This website may use essential browser technologies required for normal operation. If analytics, advertising or non-essential cookies are introduced later, the website should provide appropriate notice and consent controls where required.

We aim to use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of information handled. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The website may link to external websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

7. Data Retention

Business correspondence may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, for operational needs, or where retention is required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction or deletion of personal information. Requests can be sent using the contact details below.

9. Children's Privacy

This corporate website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

For privacy-related questions, contact info@norma.in or write to NORMA DND PRODUCTS PRIVATE LIMITED at the business address shown on our Contact page.
